# Bahawalpur Integrated Solid Waste Management (ISWM) & UC-14 Two-Bin Segregation Initiative
## Under DREAMS-I (Developing Resilient Environments and Advancing Municipal Services - Phase I)

**A Development-Sector Project Showcase & Case Study Web Platform**  
**Funding & Strategic Leadership:** Asian Development Bank (ADB, Loan No. 4396-PAK) &bull; United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF) &bull; Bahawalpur Waste Management Company (BWMC) &bull; Government of the Punjab

---

### Overview & Core Progression Pathway
This digital platform documents the transition of urban solid waste management in Bahawalpur from mixed-waste collection and open dumping to an integrated, source-segregated resource recovery system.

The core narrative follows the documented behaviour transformation framework:
$$\text{Awareness} \longrightarrow \text{Practice} \longrightarrow \text{Ownership} \longrightarrow \text{Recognition} \longrightarrow \text{Peer Influence} \longrightarrow \text{Wider Change}$$

---

### Key Milestone Achievements & Empirical Evidence
* **Pre-Inception Documentary (2024):** Commissioned 30-minute documentary (*"Film for Change: Transforming 10,000 Lives Through Stories"*), featuring character-driven waste narratives and introducing the Green (Wet) / Blue (Dry) two-bin concept.
* **Mass SBCC Outreach (2025):** 150 awareness sessions reaching **10,109 verified individuals** (Team A: 5,001; Team B: 5,108) across schools, colleges, and community centers.
* **Gender & Social Inclusion:** Women accounted for approximately **70% of participants** (6,908 out of 10,109) in community sessions, engaging directly in domestic waste sorting. Dedicated inclusive sessions were also held with the transgender community and student-led "Zero Waste Warriors."
* **UC-14 Segregation Pilot (2025–2026):** Mapped across ~101 acres in Trust Colony (~57 acres) and Mohammadia Colony (~44 acres). Over **1,035 Two-Bin sets distributed** to 1,300+ households and 91 commercial outlets.
* **Frontline Worker Empowerment:** **Rs. 22,639 in direct cash incentives** distributed to sanitary workers and supervisors from the proceeds of 550+ kg of segregated clean recyclables (184.5 kg plastic, 365.5 kg cardboard/gatta).
* **Provincial Policy Replication:** Following field visits by the Deputy Commissioner Bahawalpur, the initiative was briefed to the Chief Minister of Punjab. The Chief Minister officially directed the **replication of the UC-14 Two-Bin Segregation Model across Punjab** under the *"Suthra Punjab"* program.

### Structure of the Digital Platform (18 Comprehensive Chapters)
1. **Home:** Hero introduction, six key milestone cards, and the 6-stage transformation pathway.
2. **About DREAMS-I:** Loan No. 4396-PAK context and the core sustainability equation: $\text{Infrastructure} + \text{Systems} + \text{Institutions} + \text{Communities} + \text{Behaviour}$.
3. **Partnerships:** Connected multi-stakeholder ecosystem linking ADB, UNICEF, BWMC, Civil Administration, and Grassroots Citizens (LoU signed 5 March 2024).
4. **The Journey / Timeline:** Interactive chronological milestones from 2024 to 2026.
5. **SBCC Campaign:** 5-step behavioral framework ($\text{Inform} \to \text{Demonstrate} \to \text{Engage} \to \text{Practice} \to \text{Sustain}$) and 8 visual modalities.
6. **Inclusive Engagement:** Focus on women stewards, Zero Waste Warriors student ambassadors, and transgender community workshops.
7. **Two-Bin System & Simulator:** Green (Wet / Geela) and Blue (Dry / Sookha) standards, Urdu campaign slogan (*“سوکھے کا ٹھکانہ نیلا، ہرے میں ڈالیں گیلا”*), and interactive waste-sorting test.
8. **UC-14 Case Study (Flagship):** Comprehensive 8-phase field methodology, GIS beat mapping, and *"What the Field Taught Us"* field principles.
9. **Workers as Change Agents:** Sanitary worker empowerment, daily beat interactions, and recyclable incentive revenue-sharing.
10. **Institutional Engagement:** DC Bahawalpur field visit, CIT & CEO BWMC coordination meeting, and Commissioner/ADC General briefings.
11. **Evidence & Impact Dashboard:** Animated counters, Chart.js demographic charts, and verified municipal indicators.
12. **Interactive GIS Map:** Real Bahawalpur coordinates, UC-14 boundary overlay, beat zones, school hubs, and high-resolution mapping sheets.
13. **Documented Field Gallery:** Filterable archive of 20+ authentic project photographs with lightbox zoom and download capabilities.
14. **Documentary & Video Hub:** HTML5 video switcher featuring authentic field recordings and documentary narrative.
15. **Knowledge & Resources:** Searchable library with PDF preview modals for the signed LoU, SBCC flipbooks, checklists, and monitoring reports.
16. **Replication & Scale-Up:** Narrative of administrative briefing and high-resolution official screenshot of the Chief Minister of Punjab's directive.
17. **Lessons Learned:** Six visual cards for international and municipal practitioners.
18. **Final Chapter & Synthesis:** Closing synthesis, exportable executive summary report, and partner acknowledgments.

### How to Run and Present
The project is built entirely on modern standard web technologies (HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet.js GIS, Chart.js, and vanilla JavaScript) with **zero external compilation dependencies**:

1. **Direct Browser Access:**  
   Double-click `index.html` to open directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
2. **Local PowerShell HTTP Server:**  
   Right-click `start-server.ps1` and select *Run with PowerShell*, or run:
   ```powershell
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\start-server.ps1
   ```
   This serves the site at `http://localhost:8080/` and launches your default browser automatically.
3. **Conference / Delegation Presentation Mode:**  
   Click the **Presentation Slide Mode** button in the top navigation bar to step through each chapter full-screen using keyboard arrow keys.
4. **Print / Executive Brief Export:**  
   Click the **Print / Executive Brief** button to export a print-ready briefing document for donors, ministers, and development evaluators.
